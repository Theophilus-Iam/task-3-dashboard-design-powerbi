**Task 3: Dashboard Design**

**Project Overview**
The objective of this project was to design an interactive business dashboard that enables stakeholders to monitor performance, identify trends, and make data-driven decisions. Using Power BI, I developed a dashboard that combines KPI cards, interactive filters, trend analysis, and visual comparisons to provide a clear overview of business performance. 

**Tools Used**

* Power BI Online
* Superstore Sales Dataset
* GitHub

**Dashboard Features**

The dashboard includes:
* KPI Cards showing Total Sales and Total Profit.
* Year and Region slicers for interactive filtering.
* A time-series line chart showing sales trends over time.
* A category performance chart highlighting revenue contribution across product categories.
* A scatter plot analyzing the relationship between discounts and profitability.
* A business insights section summarising key findings and recommendations.

## Learning Approach

I adopted an interactive learning approach by combining hands-on development in Power BI with self-research and experimentation. Rather than following fixed instructions, I continuously refined the dashboard through testing, troubleshooting, and evaluating how effectively each visual communicated business insights.

This process improved my understanding of dashboard design principles, KPI selection, interactivity through slicers, and data storytelling techniques.

**Key Insights**
* Sales increased significantly between 2014 and 2017, indicating sustained business growth.
* Furniture generated the highest overall sales revenue among product categories.
* Profitability remained positive across the business, demonstrating healthy overall performance.
* Discount levels appear to influence profitability and should be monitored to prevent unnecessary margin reduction.
* Interactive filtering allows stakeholders to explore performance across different regions and time periods.

**Recommendations**
* Continue investing in high-performing product categories to sustain growth.
* Monitor discount policies to ensure profitability is maintained.
* Use regional and yearly analysis to identify high-performing market segments and replicate successful strategies.
* Expand dashboard adoption to support data-driven business decisions across departments.

**Outcome**

The final dashboard provides stakeholders with an interactive and user-friendly view of business performance. By combining KPIs, time-series analysis, comparative visuals, and interactive filters, the dashboard supports monitoring, exploration, and strategic decision-making while following dashboard design best practices.
